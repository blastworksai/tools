What it can do

  • ✓Read your files
  • ✓Take in content from outside, such as web pages or files you didn't write
  • ✓Send your data somewhere

A tool that does all three could be tricked into sending your data away. This one does all three. It also adds its own text to the AI's memory before you type, can make changes that last after you close the session, and connects to the internet.

Why it can do these

The three together are the lethal trifecta, Simon Willison's name for three things an AI tool can do. Each one is ordinary on its own. A tool that does all three can be steered by text from outside into sending your data away.
