# Rocket Fuel

A sanitized, founder-led collaboration protocol with explicitly assigned Visionary and Integrator seats.

Rocket Fuel is invoke-only. On invocation, it recommends **BASIC** or **FULL**, then asks the Operator which convergence budget to use:

- **BASIC:** at most two adversarial rounds, then Founder intervention.
- **FULL:** three rounds, swap seats for two more, then Operator extension or overrule.

The Operator's selection binds the invocation. Independent proof, review, and deployment gates remain outside those round budgets.
